Outdoor cameras fail in ordinary ways. The mount loosens. The battery gives up in a cold week. The porch light fools the motion sensor. The Wi-Fi bar that looked fine at the back door is a single bar at the back fence. A buying guide that only ranks “best outdoor” and skips those constraints is not answering the search.

This hub is for cameras that live outside the heated part of the house. The main draft is [outdoor security cameras without a subscription](/outdoor-security-cameras-without-subscription/). It stays on the version of the question that brings people here: can the camera keep clips without a cloud plan, and where do those clips sit if the camera itself is stolen?

Power is the other half of an outdoor pick. Battery cameras avoid a cable and then need a charging habit, a solar add-on, or both. Wired and Power-over-Ethernet cameras need a plan for the cable, which is a different project from pressing a mount onto siding. [Security cameras that work without Wi-Fi](/security-cameras-without-wifi/) covers the recorder end of that project. A cable to an NVR is what lets a camera keep recording through an internet outage. It does not, by itself, let you watch the camera from a phone in another city.
