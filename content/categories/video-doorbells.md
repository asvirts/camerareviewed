A video doorbell is a camera with a button, a chime problem, and a very specific piece of wall. People shop for one when a package keeps disappearing or when they want to answer the door from the grocery store. The spec sheet that matters is not only resolution. It is power, the existing chime, the Wi-Fi at the front door, and whether yesterday’s press is saved anywhere.

Three drafts cover the questions this site can answer honestly today. It does not rank a doorbell from a test this site has not done. [Do you need a Ring subscription?](/ring-subscription/) explains Ring’s public plan tiers, what Ring says still works with no plan, and what you lose when you want history. Prices on that page are dated, because Ring changes them. [How to install a wired Ring video doorbell](/install-ring-video-doorbell/) summarizes Ring’s hardwiring guidance, including the transformer ranges Ring publishes and the models Ring says are not compatible with an existing chime. It is not a diary of an installation we performed. If the breaker, the transformer, or the wiring is unfamiliar, the correct next step is a qualified electrician, not a longer blog post.

## Doorbells and monthly fees

Plenty of shoppers want a doorbell that stores events without a subscription. That search is [best video doorbell without monthly fee](/best-video-doorbell-without-monthly-fee/). It sits with the [no-subscription hub](/no-subscription/) and the camera guide, [security cameras without a subscription](/security-cameras-without-subscription/). A doorbell that records to a chime’s memory card, or to a hub inside, is a different design from Ring’s cloud history. Confirm the current model’s storage on the manufacturer’s page before you buy. The product cards on this site are still TODO placeholders.

Wired power does not remove Wi-Fi. A doorbell can take power from a low-voltage transformer and still need the home network for live view and alerts. If the front door has no usable Wi-Fi, read [security cameras that work without Wi-Fi](/security-cameras-without-wifi/) before you drill. A PoE doorbell is a different cable plan. A battery doorbell that “works anywhere” still talks to a wireless network unless you have chosen a model with another path, and those models are easy to misread on a product page.
