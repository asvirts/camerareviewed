# Content plan

Source: Google Keyword Planner export `run1.csv` (October 2025–September 2026). Volumes are Planner buckets, not exact counts. Competition index is 0–100.

Skip local-service queries (`near me`, installers, “who installs”, repair companies). The live site does not sell installation leads.

Eight guides are published. The first five are the pillars. The backlog favors buyer intent and lower competition. High-competition head terms are included only when they are the natural next page in a cluster, and the note says so.

## Clusters

- **No monthly fee:** pillars 1, the published doorbell roundup, the published wireless-system roundup, the published Arlo explainer, and the Blink, Nest, Wyze, SimpliSafe, DIY, and eufy pages.
- **Doorbells:** live Ring plan, wired install, and the no-fee roundup, plus monthly cost, voltage, height, floodlight, and the general wired-doorbell page. The non-“best” doorbell phrase is a section of the roundup, not a future URL.
- **No Wi-Fi / local recorder:** live no-Wi-Fi guide, PoE/NVR, and the “no cloud” page (that last one still uses Wi-Fi).
- **Outdoor:** live outdoor guide, the published wireless-system roundup (shared archive and radio; weather stays on the outdoor guide), plus the wireless-outdoor variant. Indoor stays a hub until there is a real indoor draft.

When a backlog page is published, add it to `content/articles/`, give it a category that already exists (or add a hub), link it from the related guides, and run `node scripts/generate-images.mjs` so the hero and OG files exist. The slug route prerenders itself.
